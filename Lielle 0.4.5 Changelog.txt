Lielle 0.4.5 Changelog

Implemented fully voiced dialogue across all major content in the 0.4.x development branch -- Some stray files may still be missing or out-of-place
Elements backported from 0.5.0:
Backported a number of scripting optimizations from 0.5.0, including central logging in custom logfile if Papyrus logging is enabled
Backported enhancements made to MCM
Backported enhancements to the spell selection framework, including support for Apocalypse - Magic of Skyrim
Backported gating elements for Lielle's Secret; Players will need to complete the College of Winterhold questline before she will study The Dreamstride
Backported a story alteration during Lielle's Secret; She will now directly ask the player their willingness to hear her out, before the nightmare starts
Backported minor dialogue revisions in Lielle's Secret
Backported dialogue revisions to her introduction dialogue and Affection dialogues
Adjusted Lielle's player-targeted healing to be more effective; temporarily disabled healing idle lines
Revised expanded follower interactions -- Some content has been removed 
Incorporated new body selection options into the FOMOD, including a new static UUNP nevernude option as the "vanilla friendly" selection -- Full README included in FOMOD
Incorporated 0.4.x High Poly Head replacer option - Requires Expressive Facial Animations -Female Edition-
Incorporated updated Clean Face warpaint remover option, with new micro-plugin to disable associated dialogue
Adjusted Lielle's appearance data, making her slightly shorter than in previous versions. Don't judge me--you know it suits her.
Adjusted Young Lielle's appearance data for consistency with above change
Made adjustments to Lielle's default AI packages so that she will now idle in the Hall of the Elements during First Lessons, as originally intended
Implemented experimental script patch for Busy Follower Framework as well as updated script patch for Extensible Follower Framework -- Note that these patches will be discontinued with 0.5.0 
Rough revisions to rebalance Affection across most of the existing content; Affection changes have now been reduced to less than whole numbers, with larger bonuses for specific quest completion -- This change is finalized more fully in 0.5.0
Backported changes made relating to various College overhauls and associated patches
Updated Obscure's CoW patch with parallel tracking feature for Lielle's door plaque
Implemented first-pass attempt at a patch for Ultimate College of Winterhold; Consider experimental until further notice
Minor edits and adjustments to patches for Immersive CoW and Magical CoW
Updated both the TK Children and Cutting Room Floor patches to ESP-FE format, to reduce plugin slot usage
Made an adjustment to the main quest commentary so that Lielle should now force greet before the player leaves for the Thalmor Embassy as part of Diplomatic Immunity
