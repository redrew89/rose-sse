Lielle 0.4.7 Changelog

 Added new falmer/dwarven dungeon location: Mnzeft
 Added new Arcane Research Quest: Field Work
 Added new Arcane Research Quest: The Anatomy of Healing
 Revised interaction with Sofia to better reflect backstory of both characters, including a new backstory element for Lielle
 Added new combat interrupt behavior when encountering frostbite spiders, including a one-time dialogue
 Removed non-free music resources 
 Removed 'Drew's Doodad' debugging item and accompanying dialogue tree. Troubleshooting options (teleport to player, reset combat state, reset AI, reset current outfit) are now in the MCM; developer testing tools are no longer included in the release
 Carried over revisions made in 0.4.x to fix scene order issues during the Gauldur Amulet quest commentary
 Minor story and dialogue additions and revisions, including a universal catch for player's who might use Legacy of the Dragonborn; now they won't lose out on an artifact for the museum
 Fixed a file structure oversight that caused a (mostly harmless) file conflict with Ashara Imperial Outfit
 Textual revisions to Lielle's Journal, to reflect additions to backstory, as well as changes to Lielle's rank at the College
 Added new dialogue within Lielle's research topic, and during the College of Winterhold questline
 Retuned the conditions on a number of idles, so they (hopefully) fire less often. Particularly, her "wait up" lines
 Textual revisions on a number of dialogues, to bring the writing style within Bethesda's established style ("Alright" vs "All right")
 Full revisions to Affection, adds Favorability as a delta metric for calculating Affection changes, scripted logic to calculate Favorability based on player choices and character creation. Affection is always kept within its current maximum, and values from older saves are carried over
 Rebalanced Affection changes via dialogue; the amount of affection gained or lost through dialogue has been reduced to less than whole integer amounts, in most cases. 
 Added expanded functionality for non-dialogue Affection events with commentary, using tracked stats to trigger reactions based on player decisions (persuasion, intimidation, bribery, using skooma or drinking excessively, etc). Responses may vary based on current Affection.
 Added patchless detection and integration for outfits from West Wind Misfit Mage and Dwemer Researcher Armor and Modular Mage; install one or both outfit mods. This feature lets Lielle dynamically change outfits based on weather, location, and specific story events. Finding the Misfit Mage chest is the first event to uncover
 Also added patchless support for Cloaks and Capes, now Lielle will equip various capes, depending on her chosen outfit, when the weather turns poor
 Note that the outfit feature offers limited player-choice in what outfit Lielle might wear, as well as an option to disable outfit switching and cloak equipping via dialogue. I plan to eventually expand it to player-provided options, for different situations, gated by Affection
 Added patchless support for KS Jewelry SSE, which enhances Lielle's wedding outfit, if installed, as well as KS Hairdos wigs component
 Added Outfit Modesty Framework, leveraging KID. Lielle will now object to outfits and armors she finds too saucy or revealing. Piggy-backs existing keyword resources, including SLA and ORF keywords. Only items the player hands to her are judged, and an ignore keyword lets specific items always be accepted
 Restructured and made slight edits to Lielle's marriage process. She will now require a two-week betrothal before marriage, as a hint for future changes to romance
 Added Dynamic Appearance, based on the initial test from 0.4.5. This release covers Lielle's glowing tattoo accent, with colour and glow options in the MCM; the accent fades in and out, and settings carry across save loads. Warpaint glow options are temporarily removed while a flickering issue with stacked RaceMenu overlays is investigated
 Added new story element for Dynamic Appearance
 Added an idle reading behavior in certain settings; implementation method borrowed from Ashe - Crystal Heart by Martimus, with permission
 Revised Lielle's follower framework, giving her complete standalone follower behavior. This makes her INCOMPATIBLE with follower framework mods like NFF/EFF/BFF/AFT
 Made further refinements and adjustments to Lielle's follower behavior. She will now automatically sandbox in certain locations while an active follower, as well as ready spells when the player draws weapons. If she falls far behind, she now catches up without interrupting scenes
 Implemented a custom home feature for post-marriage. Lielle can now be moved into any valid player home with the correct keywords, delivered through the new quest "A Place To Grow"
 Implemented a new feature to enhance Lielle's expressiveness; Emotion Manager, interfacing directly with Mfg Fix NG to provide unique expressions in dialogue. System falls back to default dialogue expressions when Mfg Fix NG is not installed, with subtle enhancements
 Added a custom eye part using the base game (or replacement) mesh with unique texture variants as part of Emotion Manager
 Added dynamic blush effect as part of Emotion Manager; depends on RaceMenu -- gracefully disables if not present. The blush starts as soon as the emotion triggers and fades in gradually
 Added animated tears effect as part of Emotion Manager; depends on RaceMenu -- gracefully falls back to shader effect method if not present. Tears appear promptly when an emotion triggers, so they register in short exchanges and with conversation camera mods, and fade in and out
 Added internal central logging and verbose notifications on start up and for specific events. These notifications can be toggled off in the MCM
 If Papyrus logging is enabled, Lielle will now write to a custom log file for debugging and testing purposes
 Fixed Lielle's Study Rank skipping rank-ups and perks when several ranks were gained at once
 Removed a large number of unused scripts left over from earlier development
 Added Affection tiers to the Affection framework. Affection now also resolves to a tier (Low, Moderate, High, or Devoted while courting or married), recalculated on every Affection change and on game load, as groundwork for simpler Affection-based dialogue
 Fixed an Affection gain in Lielle's research outfit dialogue that was never applied
 Fixed Dynamic Appearance not starting on new games
 Fixed Lielle's alternate default outfit (Modular Mage) layering over her College robes instead of replacing them
 Fixed outfit switching: asking Lielle to change into her College robes now does so even with Modular Mage installed, and the choice persists until outfits are reset; switching outfits now removes every piece of the previous one (including duplicate copies and the Modular Mage jacket), and pieces shared between outfits, such as her College boots in the Misfit outfit, are no longer stripped
 Gifts that pass Lielle's modesty check now stay on across outfit changes, giving way only where a new outfit has its own piece for that slot; armour left over from outside her outfits is taken off when she changes
 Reworked climate awareness for outfit changes: whether Lielle finds a place cold or mild now comes from where she stands on the world map, using a climate map built from the game's own weather regions (northern coasts, snowfields and mountains are cold), instead of hand-picked location lists that left most of Skyrim unclassified. Falling snow is always cold, interiors keep the last outdoor reading, and rain or nightfall bring out her cloak without an outfit change
 Fixed the MCM splash logo not appearing; the image is now sized to a multiple of 4, as its texture compression requires
 The MCM's nudity setting is now a single drop-down: Story-based (the default for new games), Always Underwear or Always Nude. The setting, and any scene nudity, now survives loading a save, and switching back to Story-based restores her undergarments
 Removed unneeded edits to vanilla records for better compatibility, including the vanilla marriage breakup dialogue, a stray script that replaced its vanilla behavior for every spouse, and spouse dialogue edits that overrode HearthfireMultiKid
 Removed an edit to the Greybeards' call story manager node that could cause excessive script stacks
 Added idle cues for the main quest and College questline commentaries: when Lielle has something to say about recent events, she'll now hint at it ("looks like she wants to say something"), until you ask her or the moment passes
 Fixed Lielle's closing dialogue for the College of Winterhold questline never appearing if the player had picked up The Dreamstride first; the College commentary no longer waits on Lielle's Secret, which already waits on the College
 Fixed Affection gain and loss notifications never appearing; their messages had come unbound from the Affection script
 Fixed the Dawnbreaker research reward not returning Dawnbreaker to the player
 Fixed Lielle's Study Rank notifications, the 'cannot study while following' notice, and two dialogue lines whose expressions never triggered
 Fixed choosing most player homes after the wedding doing nothing; Lielle now moves to any of the vanilla houses, from the first post-wedding conversation or when asked to move later, and moving her clears any custom home set up through "A Place To Grow"
